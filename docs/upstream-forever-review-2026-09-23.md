# Forever mechanics review — 23 September 2026

Reviewed the first four pages of the [`wowsims/forever` master history](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commits/master/), pinned at [`08dfb6be22a0`](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/08dfb6be22a0) (client build 1.60.1.69977). This covered approximately 400 recent commits, from 16–23 September. Older page-five entries mostly precede the Forever port. Read the combat-relevant commit diffs, compared their pinned tree with our engine, and checked relevant 69977 client rows and the three [`magey/forever-warrior` issues](https://github.com/magey/forever-warrior/issues). This is not an assertion that every upstream implementation is an observed server rule.

## Already modeled or irrelevant to this DPS encounter

- [Rend's periodic crit](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/872e6def91c1): `sim/core/ruleset.go` already allows physical DoT ticks to crit in Forever. Prior Arms results recorded Rend critical ticks; another Rend crit patch would be a duplicate.
- [Boundless Rage, Raging Blows and Improved Cleave](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/8b18758cb0): our cap, off-hand Whirlwind gate and rage-cost discount already match these talent descriptions.
- [Battle Shout's Forever row](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/fce411e29400): our rank-seven 139 AP and three-minute duration were already corrected.
- [Retribution Aura spell-power scaling](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/48dbee2620fd) has a level-20 observation behind it. The current reference boss never attacks DPS players, so external retaliation contributes no damage to this matrix. It remains a simulator gap for tank/solo scenarios, especially because an external aura needs the **provider's** spell power rather than the recipient's.
- The branch's [race/class stat review](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/0c8e81e0ab) explicitly warns that **its primary attributes are still level-70 values on a level-60 character**. Do not copy them into our level-60 profiles. Our own baseline attributes also need an independent Forever level-60 check; see T51.

## Conflicts and open mechanics

- [WoWSims removed the queue hit-table effect](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/69fee287068a), then [made Heroic Strike/Cleave instant off-GCD specials](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/4c32f399aa1e). But 69977 `SpellMisc.Attributes_0` on Heroic Strike 78/25286 and Cleave 845 is `0x50014`, retaining the on-next-swing `0x4` bit; Slam 11605 and Bloodthirst 23894 lack it (`0x50010`). The later in-game [queued off-hand observation](https://github.com/magey/forever-warrior/issues/2) supports an actual queue. We retain next-swing replacement and provisionally restore the observed off-hand miss benefit; no instantaneous free extra attacks are added. Recheck against a controlled combat log at higher levels (T50).
- [Attack-table issue #1](https://github.com/magey/forever-warrior/issues/1) lists measurements to make, not a settled hit/glancing formula. No attack-table formula was replaced.
- [WoWSims changed Rend/Deep Wounds to dynamic ticks](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/36cfc58328e3). Its commit describes non-snapshotting as a rule but supplies no controlled Forever comparison; Rend's source row also has no explicit weapon/AP coefficient. Our current snapshot behavior therefore remains an explicit test question rather than silently adopting an uncertain difference. Compare an AP/damage buff applied *mid-bleed* (T52).
- [WoWSims adjusted Weaponmaster's main-hand equipment gate and armor-ignore stacking](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/436f0c1cc233). Our off-hand mace can currently grant a character-wide armor discount, while their branch requires a main-hand mace. None of the current Warrior ranking profiles uses that combination. Client talent text alone does not resolve the off-hand case; keep it out of candidate optimizations until tested. Sword procs already check the hitting hand here.
- [WoWSims' incoming rage update](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/f9f9f2188324) doubles the new damage-based award under Berserker Rage. The client says “extra rage” but does not state its magnitude; our older flat bonus and their 2× multiplier are both unresolved at level 60. Do not treat either as verified without a before/after log.

The matched post-change results and the prior release are kept separately. In particular, a DPS delta under the new mechanics does not prove that every extrapolated level-60 rule is right.

## Matched five-minute replay

All 171 race/build profiles and their Tier-off, +10% and +50% equipment scenarios were rerun at 5,000 iterations per arm: 684 successful runs, no APL warnings. Against the archived preceding release, Retribution averaged **+66.11 DPS** across its three races, Fury **−5.98 DPS** and two-handed Fury **−4.72 DPS** across ten races each. Every other build's mean changed by less than 0.5 DPS. These are combined corrections, not isolated estimates of a single ability. In particular, Ret now banks the client's separate Command/Righteousness Echoes, and its preset tests the corresponding aura IDs instead of the obsolete shared placeholder. [Current matrix and raw requests/results](https://github.com/gunba/wow-forever-sim/blob/forever/artifacts/forever_dps_5min.json); [pre-correction snapshot](https://github.com/gunba/wow-forever-sim/blob/forever/artifacts/history/67e20d8c3/README.md).
