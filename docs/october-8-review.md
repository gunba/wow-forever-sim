# October 8 evidence review

## Sources and coverage

The review starts from publication `684f9d6cdc1782de40313b0125020c65d1680b6e`. Its untouched results and inputs are [archived](../artifacts/correctness/october_8/before.json.gz). The current benchmark integrates the corrections and bounded selections below; historical results remain tied to their recorded engines.

- The [October 1 development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696/4) were last edited October 2 at 02:55 UTC, before that publication. October 5–6 maintenance notices add service fixes, not new combat coefficients.
- The new [Rogue/Warlock deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock) was published October 7 at 22:00 UTC. It clarifies existing behavior as well as exposing omissions; its publication does not date every mechanic change.
- Static client builds `1.60.1.70170` and `1.60.1.70245` were compared across 34 tables. Thirty-three are byte-identical. `PlayerExpectedStat` only renames a column to `HPPerStamina`; all row values remain identical. Static equality does **not** imply unchanged server scripts or hotfixes.
- The new class-channel review covers **284 actual messages in 11 visible channels**, October 1 at 01:00 UTC through October 8 at 10:17 UTC: general 32, Druid 4, Hunter 25, Mage 37, Paladin 29, Priest 20, Rogue 35, Shaman 89, Warlock 9, Warrior 4 and announcements 0. Every message has a local disposition. Raw text, identities, reply references and media remain private.
- Visible-channel pagination and permitted thread searches completed. Hidden channels, unjoined private threads and some cross-server replies were inaccessible. Linked `wowsims/forever` PR 283 and issue 30 returned 404, including authenticated access; their current diffs were not reviewed.
- Public upstream research is pinned to ElliotWood/Forever `56c11f4e2bc69caa5d7995aaf0ffb0e68466c730`. All 305 commits and 938 file sections were classified. All non-generated runtime changed lines and 94 relevant APL/preset/harness sections were read; generated database/ranking rows were not independently validated row by row. Final dispositions include 79 runtime reviews, 137 duplicate changelog records, 29 data corroborations, 14 unsupported-feature changes, 43 configuration/APL leads and three documentation/test changes. Another implementation is a lead, not independent game evidence.

### Ice Lance calibration

Public reports [2727](https://foreverlogs.gg/reports/2727) and [2687](https://foreverlogs.gg/reports/2687) were captured as processed event timelines, not merely accepted through [PR 726](https://github.com/ElliotWood/Forever/pull/726).

In report 2727, 52 normal-looking rank-one hits range from 36–42 with mean 38.77; 32 high/frozen hits divided by four average 38.15. Contemporaneous Frostbolt ranks independently suggest approximately 80–81 SP. With the client's level-capped base range, a 0.143 share predicts a minimum near 39, whereas twelve observed hits were 36–37. About 0.10–0.11 fits better. The smaller second report is consistent with that range.

This is not a controlled SP-delta experiment. Detailed talent allocations, some equipment effects, resistance/absorb fields and exact frozen state are incomplete. Five unusual damage values in the first report remain in the evidence rather than being silently erased. Higher ranks still need a controlled slope test.

### Further runtime corrections

- **Missile Barrage:** eligible hits must land before the proc roll. Cast completion and misses no longer grant it; existing chances remain unchanged.
- **Master of Elements:** free casts receive the sourced base-cost refund. A 9ms cooldown prevents simultaneous AoE hits from repeatedly refunding one batch.
- **Frostfire DoT:** Arcane Power and Arcane Instability periodic masks exclude it; Piercing Ice has a flat 2% periodic effect, unlike its ranked direct effect. Direct stacking is unchanged.
- **Blizzard:** initial Arcane Concentration rolls are implemented without inventing damage or generic hit callbacks. A proposed Arcane Missiles exclusion was withheld: its child rows explicitly carry `NOT_A_PROC`, conflicting with the weak no-proc sample.
- **Lightning Overload:** all 14 ranks use their own client ranges, capped growth and coefficients, not half of rounded parent values. At level 60 the highest CL child mean is 66.8 rather than 63.25; highest LB is 100.4 rather than 100.5. Rank-three CL also has its own 0.2855 SP share. Proper triggered Overload crits no longer grant Elemental Devastation. Ordinary/Windfury Flurry semantics were not guessed from a passive flag.
- **Resource threat:** Life Tap, including demon mana transfers, and Dark Sacrifice no longer acquire phantom mana-gain threat. Their resource amounts are unchanged. Charge remains unregistered; this is not a new Charge or rage-formula implementation.
- **Volley:** melee was already blocked during its channel. Removed only the fixed six-second ranged delay, so hasted completion and early clipping resume the existing Auto Shot timing, including its 500ms wind-up.
- **Caster Striking enchants:** the equipment search reproduced a crash when a legal enchant updated a weapon without a melee autoattack handler. Weapon data now updates without dereferencing or creating that handler. All five Striking ranks, caster equip/swap and existing Feral/Warrior reconstruction checks pass. Completed searches were preserved; only unfinished jobs resume on the corrected engine.

Each correction above has a reproduced failure and a passing focused regression. Source review also rejected apparent Screech, trap-cooldown and Holy Nova gaps: the existing Forever dispatch paths already implement them. The other engine's completed-cast batching/pushback issue is not present in this engine's completion architecture.

A recheck of the SoR claim found approximately **0.222 flat**, not a coefficient multiplied by weapon speed. Local effective two-handed scaling is about 0.22 because the coefficient enters twice. Captured low-level observations cannot distinguish that rounding difference or fully resolve self-SP versus target-bonus scaling. No new SoR coefficient was imported.

## Focused comparisons

The first comparison phase used the corrected engine, fixed published gear/talents/APLs and ordinary MP5. It screened 1,032 legal temporary-enchant/stone configurations, then ran 338 confirmation arms using two independent 5,000-iteration seeds (`1791460001` and `1791480001`). Their selected recipes are integrated into the current rankings; engine-specific controls remain in the evidence archives.

- **All 27 Rogue profiles:** Shadow Oil on the main hand plus an Elemental Sharpening Stone on the off hand improved DPS by about **19–22** versus the same corrected-engine profiles without those temporary enchants. Poisons remain independently equipped. Some nearby configurations are statistically close; the common recipe avoids treating screen noise as a race-specific optimum.
- **All 20 Warlock profiles:** adding a legal stone alongside the existing oil improved DPS. Spellstone was selected for Affliction, Demonic Pact and DS/Ruin; Firestone for Destruction. Gains against no stone were about **20–28 DPS**. The smaller Firestone/Spellstone difference for Destruction is not a claim of universal superiority.
- **Mutilate timing:** 72 further arms covered all nine races, four Cold Blood conditions and two independent 5,000-iteration seeds (`1791520001`, `1791540001`). Reserving Cold Blood for Mutilate lost about **1.8–2.2 DPS** versus the existing finisher timing. Moving the finisher threshold from four to three combo points gave no supported gain. The existing priority is retained; fixing both-hand eligibility does not automatically make Mutilate its best use.

Each retained enchant/stone gain cleared the pooled unknown-covariance bound and was positive on both seeds. For each matched seed, the error bound uses the sum of both arm SEs; independent seed-pair bounds are then combined in quadrature. Earlier independence approximations remain recorded as such, not mislabeled as guaranteed conservative bounds. No equipment or talent change was used to manufacture those gains. The later gear/APL studies retain these controls and account for new weapon choices. Demonic Pact's stone comparison was refreshed after the Brand crit correction; all five races retained Spellstone.

A separate 160-arm Hawk comparison used the same logical **one-Hawk maintenance policy**, migrating only the obsolete DoT predicate to a guardian-count query. The corrected model lowered total DPS by about **38–41** across Beast Mastery and Pet/Melee profiles. Within the chosen 0.30–0.40 damage / 1–3% effective-crit scenarios, total DPS moved by roughly **−2 to +2** around the calibration. Adding the intended Ferocity assault bonus as a separate sensitivity added about **1.8 DPS** for Beast Mastery. These ranges are scenario comparisons, **not bounds on all possible unknown server behavior**, especially unmeasured AP transfer and high-rank scaling.

The guardian change also exposed an invalid old APL query that could panic. Missing-DoT queries now produce a validation warning; Hunter APLs have explicit active-Hawk-count and earliest-expiry values. One-Hawk controls remain archived. Another 224 arms independently confirmed the selected policies: two-Hawk refresh for Beast Mastery gains **0.7–2.7 DPS** under the central calibration; Pet/Melee cooldown spending gains **20–22 DPS**, retaining its gain in low/high parameter scenarios. The Beast Mastery improvement is **not supported under the tested low calibration**. The isolated central-model APL gain also does not clear the stronger covariance bound for High Order/Windshaper. Their subsequently selected talent-plus-APL combination was therefore tested against the original one-Hawk/talent control: both improve by about **3.6 DPS**, exceeding the **0.85 DPS** bound on two independent seeds. The defaults remain conditional model choices, not verified server optima.

## Profile and result integration

Affected selection comparisons use the applicable corrected engine on **both** arms and independent confirmation seeds. The [selection index](../artifacts/correctness/october_8/selection-evidence.json) links complete native-only evidence packets, engine phases, exact inputs and uncertainty calculations. Raw game/chat captures are not published.

- **Mage:** all six Arcane profiles use a three-stack Frostbolt reset, gaining about 5.6–7.6 DPS. Five Fire races move one point from Precision to Meditation, gaining about 2.9–6.7 DPS despite losing 0.9–1 percentage point of hit; High Order retains its original allocation. Arcane–Frost and Frost remain unchanged.
- **Warrior/Shaman:** Fury and Fury (Sunder) move Enrage 5→4 and Improved Overpower 1→2; 2H Bloodthirst moves Blood Craze 3→2 for Raging Blows; Stormcaller moves Ancestral Knowledge 4→5 and Elemental Weapons 3→2. All 35 retained talent changes clear the final bound; Arms, Elemental and Enhancement talents remain unchanged.
- **Hunter:** all eight Beast Mastery races move Improved Stings 3→2 and Efficiency 1→2; Pet/Melee moves Resourcefulness 2→1 for Rapid Killing. The selected Hawk policies remain qualified as described above. Marksmanship stays petless and its APL is unchanged.
- **Rogue gear:** 13 of 27 independently confirmed proposals are retained, about 1.3–7.7 DPS. Combat Orc uses the legal one-handed axe off hand; Mutilate remains dual-dagger. The existing Mutilate Cold Blood timing is retained.
- **Other affected DPS gear:** 145 profiles across 23 builds were searched with the original modeled-only coordinate selector: 100-iteration screens, 1,000-iteration coordinate validation and at most three passes. All 580 final confirmation arms used the corrected release-2 engine. Thirty-five proposals cleared positive-both-seeds and the no-assumed-covariance 95% bound, gaining about 1.7–14.0 DPS; 110 original choices remain. Fury/Dwarf reached the three-pass limit **without convergence**, but its separately confirmed gain cleared the bound. No extra pass or global-optimum claim is made. Forty-seven completed searches on the earlier equivalent engine were preserved; only 98 unfinished/failed searches resumed after the caster Striking crash fix. No legal candidate was excluded to avoid that crash.
- **Protection Paladin gear:** all three races pass the original guards on each seed and **both** workloads: DTPS ≤+1%, TMI ≤+2%, death probability ≤+.0025 absolute, TPS ≥−1%, least-target TPS ≥−2%. Single-target DPS gains are about 45–50; multi-target gains about 95–107. These choices spend some defensive tolerance: **TMI increases slightly**. Passing point-estimate guards is not a 95% proof of no harm, and no survival improvement is claimed. Talents, APL, duties, healing, support, Titans, oil and Blacksmithing/Engineering stay fixed. An initial flag-routing pilot captured baseline pairs only and is archived separately, not counted as a search. Protection Warrior and Bear gear remain fixed.
