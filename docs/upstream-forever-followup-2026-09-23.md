# WoWSims Forever follow-up — 23 September 2026

Compared [`08dfb6be`…`9e7fd623`](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/compare/08dfb6be22a0...9e7fd62313cb), 225 additional commits, with the published simulator at [`7fe1b536`](https://github.com/gunba/wow-forever-sim/commit/7fe1b5366). Most of this range builds a client-spell-data store and moves class code to its accessors; it does not supply 225 observed changes to combat rules. The current branch still has unimplemented class bodies, including [Aspect of the Hawk](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/9e7fd623/sim/hunter/aspects.go), so its generated rows cannot be substituted wholesale for working class simulations.

| Upstream change | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| [Sapper self-damage is its own spell hit](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/8fb1a2d7). | Our sapper already deals a spell hit to its user. Warrior Enrage, Blood Craze and incoming Rage listen to that hit without requiring the outgoing spell's proc mask. All three Warrior benchmark rows use a sapper; this is worth keeping covered, but not a missing DPS effect in our engine. |
| [Rupture uses the combo points spent at cast](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/3de7a590). | Our Rupture damage and duration receive the cast's combo points. The upstream edit was in a commented-out port body. Already modeled. |
| [Deadly Poison ticks on its current stack count](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/3202a454). | Our tick base damage increments when a stack is applied, so the next tick includes it. Our multiplier snapshots on the first stack, unlike their commented-out dynamic-tick example. Whether Forever updates other damage multipliers mid-DoT still needs a controlled test; do not infer that rule from a port stub. |
| [Focused Rage discounts Demoralizing Shout](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/a7aaa84a). | Our talent discounts offensive Warrior spells only. The client-mask difference concerns a Protection utility shout; the upstream report says no DPS-suite key moved. None of our current DPS profiles casts it with Focused Rage. A simulator gap outside this benchmark, not a reason to move the ranking. |
| [Overpower/Revenge and Pummel/Shield Bash share client cooldown categories](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/d005931e). | Our engine gives the paired abilities separate timers. No current DPS rotation casts both members of either pair. Record for the wider simulator; it does not change the present encounter. |
| [Item and enchant proc-store overhaul](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/5237e7b8). | Of the 95 distinct items worn in the **pinned 7fe1b5366** profiles, only Frozen Heart and Molten Heart have active registrations in the upstream Forever item-effect files; our simulator already registers both. Weakness Analyzer appears in the upstream generated list as **unsupported/commented out**, not a verified substitute for its sourced implementation here. The overhaul does not establish new working effects for the other selected gear. |

The branch's instant [Heroic Strike/Cleave](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/4c32f399aa1e) remains incompatible with the client's on-next-swing bit and the [queued off-hand observation](https://github.com/magey/forever-warrior/issues/2). We continue to model them as queued. The [earlier review](upstream-forever-review-2026-09-23.md) records the accepted fixes and open level-60 tests. No new DPS rule was imported solely from this follow-up range.

## Equipment state

The branch commits an [item database](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/tree/9e7fd623/assets/database), but no `*.gear.json` or `gear_sets/` files in its UI tree at this revision. The [Warrior test](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/9e7fd623/sim/warrior/dps/dps_test.go) still names `ui/specs/warrior/dps/gear_sets/p1_fury`, `p4_fury` and others, despite those files not being present. These references are not usable optimized level-60 Forever sets. Our source-reviewed item pool and measured gear selections remain independent of them.
